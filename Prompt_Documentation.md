# HerHealth AI – Prompt Engineering Documentation

## Prompt 1 – Product idea
Design a practical women's healthcare education website for pregnancy care and PCOS awareness. It must be a working interactive prototype, not a static page. Identify the user problem, target audience, features and safety limits.

## Prompt 2 – Feature specification
Build a Streamlit website with pregnancy LMP-based timeline estimate, appointment organizer, PCOS education, cycle and symptom journal, optional AI explainer, trusted resources and CSV export. Use a soft feminine lavender, peach and white visual style.

## Prompt 3 – Multilingual UX
Add English, Hindi and Marathi language selection. Translate the main navigation, pregnancy labels, PCOS journal labels, safety notice and AI explainer interface. Ensure the AI answers in the selected language. Keep medical terms clear and do not claim that every medical concept has a perfect direct translation.

## Prompt 4 – Pregnancy safety refinement
Use a simple 280-day LMP calculation only as an estimate. State the 28-day cycle assumption and explain that a clinician may revise dating using history and ultrasound. Do not provide a fixed personalized checkup schedule. Allow users to record appointments recommended by their own maternity-care team.

## Prompt 5 – PCOS safety refinement
Provide general PCOS education and a cycle/symptom journal. Do not diagnose PCOS from symptoms, provide a diagnostic score, prescribe medication or supplements, or recommend restrictive diets. Encourage clinical assessment for personal concerns.

## Prompt 6 – AI system prompt
You are HerHealth AI, a cautious women's-health education assistant. Respond in the selected language. Give general educational information in plain language. Do not diagnose, determine whether the user has PCOS or another condition, prescribe or recommend medicines/supplements, provide individualized treatment, or replace a qualified clinician. Explain that pregnancy schedules and tests depend on local guidance and individual circumstances. LMP date calculations are estimates only. Explain that PCOS symptoms overlap with other conditions and diagnosis requires clinician assessment. Do not infer diagnoses from symptoms. Encourage professional advice for personal concerns. For urgent or severe symptoms, advise contacting local emergency services or an appropriate healthcare professional promptly. Do not request identifying information. State uncertainty clearly.

## Prompt 7 – Final assessment review
Review the app for practical usefulness, AI integration, prompt iteration, technical execution, accessibility, language support, privacy and medical safety. Identify remaining limitations and propose a demo sequence.

## Iteration summary
- Started with a single-language women's health idea.
- Added distinct pregnancy and PCOS workflows.
- Added English, Hindi and Marathi interface strings and language-aware AI responses.
- Added a clinician-led appointment organizer rather than a prescriptive schedule.
- Restricted AI to general education and explicitly prohibited diagnosis and treatment advice.
- Added privacy warnings and session-only tracking for the prototype.
